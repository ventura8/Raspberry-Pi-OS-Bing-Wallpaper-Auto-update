# Workspace Instructions

Use this repository policy on all changes:

1. Quality checks are mandatory before tests.
2. Do not add lint or type-check suppressions in source code.
3. Keep non-Markdown source/config line length at 140 or fewer.
4. Keep CI and local quality checks in parity.
5. Preserve coverage threshold of 90% or greater.
